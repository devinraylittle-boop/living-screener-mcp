MANDATORY PRE-TRADE CHECK
Before placing any live-cash autonomous order, verify:
1. Stage 5 readiness says GO_LIVE_CASH_AUTONOMY.
2. Broker account, buying power, positions, open orders, and restrictions have been reconciled.
3. There are no duplicate open orders and no existing open order for the same symbol.
4. Market data is live, valid, fresh, and consistent across the scanner and broker quote.
5. The symbol is in the allowed universe and is regular-session tradeable.
6. Spread is at or below 35 basis points.
7. Liquidity is sufficient for the planned order.
8. No halt, LULD issue, major unresolved news shock, platform outage, or abnormal price feed exists.
9. Position size, total exposure, correlated exposure, and risk per trade are within limits.
10. Stop-loss, invalidation level, and exit logic are defined before entry.
11. Expected reward-to-risk is at least 2.0:1.
12. The trade does not violate legal, regulatory, broker, exchange, account, cash-settlement, or pattern-day-trading restrictions.
13. The trade is not based on insider information, market manipulation, spoofing, layering, wash trading, or any deceptive practice.

ORDER EXECUTION RULES
- Prefer marketable limit orders with whole shares.
- Use fractional/dollar market orders only during regular market hours when a whole-share limit order is not practical, the symbol is highly liquid, spread is tight, and notional is within limit.
- Never submit an order without a defined exit plan.
- Submit or maintain protective stop/risk-reduction logic immediately where the broker route supports it.
- Cancel stale unfilled orders after 5 minutes or sooner if price drift exceeds 0.30%.
- Use a unique idempotency key for every logical order and reuse the same key only for retries of that same logical order.
- Confirm every fill, partial fill, rejection, cancellation, and position update against broker state.

POSITION MANAGEMENT
- Never widen a stop to avoid taking a loss.
- Move stops only to reduce risk or protect gains when justified by the approved strategy.
- Close or reduce when the thesis invalidates, liquidity deteriorates, market data becomes unreliable, limits are approached, or broker state cannot be reconciled.
- Close before market close unless overnight holding conditions are explicitly satisfied.
- Closing one position to open another is allowed only when closing is independently justified and the replacement candidate still passes all checks on the next cycle.

KILL SWITCH CONDITIONS
Immediately stop opening new live-cash trades, cancel pending orders where safe, and flatten or risk-reduce open positions if any occur:
- Daily, weekly, or total drawdown limit is reached.
- Broker/API connection becomes unstable.
- Market data is stale, inconsistent, delayed, or corrupted.
- Broker confirmations do not match internal state.
- Duplicate order, unexpected position, rejected protective order, settlement issue, PDT warning, margin/leverage risk, or account restriction appears.
- Slippage exceeds 0.30%.
- More than 2 consecutive live-cash losses occur.
- Strategy behavior deviates from tested assumptions.
- Any legal, regulatory, compliance, or data-integrity concern appears.

STRATEGY DISCIPLINE
Only promoted, documented strategies may trade live cash. No new live strategy may be invented during market hours. Parameter changes require separate review and must not be made to justify a trade.
