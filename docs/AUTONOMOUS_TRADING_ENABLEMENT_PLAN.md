# Autonomous Trading Enablement Plan

## Current Live-Cash Lane

Approved first lane: Robinhood Agentic equity only.

Overall system readiness is defined in `docs/PREMIUM_AUTONOMOUS_TRADING_SYSTEM_PLAN.md` and machine gates are defined in `config/autonomous_readiness_gates.json`.

Blocked lanes:

- Robinhood options: wait for option tools to be exposed.
- Robinhood crypto: wait for crypto tools to be exposed or add another crypto executor.
- Alpaca live: validated, but do not use for autonomous cash until the paper lane and risk telemetry are clean.
- Alpaca paper: validated against `paper-api.alpaca.markets` on 2026-06-16. Use it aggressively for research and data gathering.

## Initial Cash Automation Limits

These are deliberately small until the system proves execution quality:

- Broker: Robinhood Agentic account ending 6199.
- Asset class: equities only.
- Direction: long only.
- Order type: marketable limit preferred; no blind market entries outside a deliberate test.
- Max order notional: 10 USD.
- Max open positions: 1 until first live cycle is reviewed.
- Max daily trades: 3.
- Max daily loss: 5 USD hard stop.
- Per-trade stop risk: target 0.50 USD or less.
- No averaging down.
- No new entries if there is any open order.
- No new entries if market data, broker review, tradability, buying power, positions, or order history checks fail.

## Required Proof Before First Live Order

1. Portfolio and buying power fetched.
2. Open positions fetched.
3. Open orders fetched.
4. Candidate generated by scanner with evidence packet.
5. Candidate is equity, long, tradable, and liquid.
6. Quote is fresh.
7. Limit price is near bid/ask, not intentionally unmarketable.
8. Broker `review_equity_order` passes with no blocking alerts.
9. Risk check passes max loss, max position, max trade count, and duplicate-order checks.
10. A trade journal entry is prepared before placement.
11. Explicit live launch phrase is supplied by operator for the first live order.

## Aggressive Paper Research

Run paper research aggressively, but keep it separate from cash gates:

- Log every paper entry and close.
- Include entry timestamp, asset class, strategy, evidence snapshot, entry price, stop, target, exit price, exit reason, slippage estimate, and P/L.
- Allow exploratory/bad setups only in paper.
- Use paper results to propose rules, not to auto-change live gates.
- Use `docs/BAD_SETUP_LEARNING_SYSTEM.md` as the blueprint for counterfactual bad-setup learning.
- Use `docs/ANOMALY_STRATEGY_ROADMAP.md` and `config/anomaly_strategy_registry.json` to prioritize anomaly lanes.
